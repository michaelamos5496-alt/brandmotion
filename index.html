The Studio

BrandMotion Studios

We started BrandMotion Studios with a single conviction: that African stories, told honestly and shot beautifully, belong in front of the world.

Six years later, that conviction has taken us to Vogue editorials, United Airlines documentaries, fashion campaigns with internationally recognised designers, and development films with talent from the biggest shows on Netflix. We've worked across Ghana, West Africa, and three continents — bringing the same eye to every frame.

We are a director-led studio. That means every project we take on is treated as a film — not a deliverable. The brief matters. The story matters. The feeling it leaves behind matters most of all.

Our base is Accra. Our reach is everywhere.
